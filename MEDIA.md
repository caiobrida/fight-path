The [Capcom video policy](https://press.capcom.com/Video-Policy) was inspected. It supports qualifying educational footage with conditions; it is not a blanket redistribution license for official art or other creators' work. No unreviewed gameplay clips were added. Add future captured clips only after recording source/build, permission scope, captions and demonstrable route conditions.

The player follows [YouTube embed parameters](https://developers.google.com/youtube/player_parameters) and [privacy-enhanced embedding](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/171780). No iframe, thumbnail fetch, preconnect or remote video request exists before explicit loading. Autoplay is disabled; loading and playing are separate actions. Closing unmounts the iframe. The no-cookie domain reduces personalization; it does not eliminate Google data processing. A source link remains usable when embedding fails. No transcript is claimed without verification; the original written lesson provides a separate teaching alternative. All illustrations are static, including under reduced-motion settings.
