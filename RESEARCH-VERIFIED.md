## What is verified

Capcom's own [Drive System introduction](https://news.capcomusa.com/2022/06/02/street-fighter-6-redefines-the-genre-in-2023/) by Andy Wong describes five Drive techniques, six Drive stocks, and Burnout when the gauge is exhausted. This is first-party publication from June 2, 2022, not a current patch specification. It supports a high-level systems lesson; do not derive 2026 costs, exact timings, damage or punish windows from it.

The [PlayStation article authored by Capcom's Andy Wong](https://blog.playstation.com/2022/06/02/street-fighter-6-aims-to-redefine-the-fighting-genre-in-2023/) independently explains the same system: Impact is an armored strike with wall interaction, Parry repels attacks, Rush advances from Parry or a normal, Overdrive enhances specials, and Reversal is a counterattack. Published June 2, 2022; updated October 11, 2022. Its explanations can anchor introductory copy while current detailed rules remain subject to patch review.

Capcom also describes the game's [tutorials, character guides and training features](https://blog.playstation.com/2023/04/20/street-fighter-6-showcase-new-gameplay-details-future-fighters-revealed-and-demo-launched/) in its April 20, 2023 showcase article. Sending learners to SF6 training mode is appropriate; a FightPath motion result cannot verify hits or cancels in that environment.

The official [Ken Character Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1SX2m7sqbtA), published May 18, 2023 on the verified Street Fighter channel, describes space control with kicks and an aggressive pressure plan. Its metadata and description were inspected. Exact scene timings, BnB inputs and current frame data have not been checked from video playback in this update.

Capcom's [August 3, 2026 announcement](https://news.capcomusa.com/lets/browse/activate-bayani-mode-as-yasmine-launches-today-in-street-fighter-6) says that update includes a battle balance change. That establishes the existence of a 2026 revision, not its complete changes or the latest build on November 17. No exact current build number has been verified. Pre-2026 recipes must not be tagged as current-patch tested merely because their source remains online.

| [Infil: Footsies](https://glossary.infil.net/?t=Footsies) | Contest space and seek favorable ranges while limiting the opponent's options | Broad concept; varies by game and is not exclusively walking on the ground |
| [Infil: Neutral](https://glossary.infil.net/?t=Neutral) | Neither side is currently being hit or blocking, and players seek a way to begin their plan | Distinguish from pressure and wake-up situations |
| [Infil: Whiff Punish](https://glossary.infil.net/?t=Whiff%20Punish) | Attack the recovery of an opponent's missed move | Examples need visible range and timing, without invented frame values |
| [Infil: Hit Confirm](https://glossary.infil.net/?t=Hit%20Confirm) | Continue an attack sequence after recognizing a hit; avoid unsafe follow-through on block | Not every starter is practically confirmable for every learner |
