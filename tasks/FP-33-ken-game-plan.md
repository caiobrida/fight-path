# FP-33 — Build Ken Classic's beginner game plan

**Status:** Awaiting owner approval. **Priority:** P2. **Dependencies:** FP-31, FP-32.

## Checklist

- [ ] Choose a small verified toolkit for range control, anti-air, conversion, pressure and defense; explain why each tool is selected.
- [ ] Teach approaching, confirming, ending a turn, corner decisions and adaptation to repeated opponent choices.
- [ ] Explain risk/context for run, Jinrai and Dragonlash where covered; do not turn offensive identity into advice to attack continuously.
- [ ] Link official guide scenes and current move/frame references; label strategic recommendations as contextual teaching advice.
- [ ] Provide drills and common mistakes at beginner level, tied to later BnB situations rather than a raw move encyclopedia.

**Acceptance:** learners can describe a simple Ken plan and when to stop pressure, defend or change a repeated option. Every move-specific claim has current evidence and both translations.

**Evidence:** reviewed toolkit, timestamps, build scope and a training/replay example for each decision.
