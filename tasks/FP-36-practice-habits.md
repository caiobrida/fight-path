# FP-36 — Teach practice habits and adaptation

**Status:** Approved for implementation (2026-11-17). **Priority:** P2. **Dependencies:** FP-29, FP-30; FP-33 for Ken examples.

## Checklist

- [ ] Add lessons for one goal per session, focused repetition, recognizing repeated habits, reviewing a round and testing a response.
- [ ] Explain attention/mental-stack tradeoffs and context-dependent aggression/defense using authored sources and concrete scenarios.
- [ ] Provide short session templates: observe a problem, choose a drill, try it, note what changed and reassess.
- [ ] Include Ken-specific examples of choosing a range, defending after a turn and confirming rather than repeating unsafe options.
- [ ] Avoid guarantees about rank, psychological outcomes or fixed prescriptions; keep any personal notes optional and local unless separately designed under PRIVACY.md.

**Acceptance:** learners can set and evaluate a concrete practice goal without treating win rate as the only sign of progress. Content is source-backed and understandable in both locales.

**Evidence:** bilingual editorial review and example practice sessions.
